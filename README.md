# news-image-20261125 — The world on Wednesday 25 November 2026

A one-page newspaper front page that sums up Wednesday 25 November 2026
in six stories, drawn as a single image. An AI judge compares it against
other entries for the day; the 50 best share $100.

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125

## What the image shows

- **Lead — China launches Shenzhou-24.** A Long March rocket lifts off
  from Jiuquan with three crew, including Pakistani Muhammad Zeeshan Ali,
  the first foreign astronaut to visit the Tiangong space station.
- **CIA and the spy chief's office hit by a hack.** CNN reports a
  cyberattack on the CIA and the Director of National Intelligence;
  classified systems were said to be unaffected.
- **Russia calls ship strikes "terrorism".** Moscow warns of "severe
  consequences" after Ukrainian drone attacks on a cargo ship in the Gulf
  of Finland and a warship off Libya.
- **WHO pulls staff from an Ebola-zone hospital.** Health workers are
  evacuated from a hospital in Ituri, DR Congo, after three days
  sheltering from fighting, amid the country's worst Ebola outbreak.
- **Trump readies a new tariff wave.** Washington prepares fresh duties
  on China, the EU and others, replacing levies struck down by the
  Supreme Court.
- **Also:** Kilmar Ábrego García leaves the US for Spain; India declares
  itself free of the Naxalite insurgency.

Every story above happened or was first reported on 25 November 2026,
as reported that day by Reuters, the BBC, CNN, Xinhua and the WHO. The
image is drawn with flat colours and large type so it stays legible at
about 300 pixels across.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the `entry.png`
held in this repository to the output directory — `/output/entry.png`,
or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set. It runs with no network and writes
identical bytes on every run.

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
